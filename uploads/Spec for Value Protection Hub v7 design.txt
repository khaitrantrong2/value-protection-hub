## Asset strategy

Do not pretend that CSS or primitive Three.js geometry can reproduce premium character art.

For this iteration, use one of these approaches in order of preference:

### Preferred implementation for current UI pass

Use high-quality transparent character renders in WebP or PNG format.

Requirements:
- one consistent art direction for all ten people
- transparent background
- full-body pose
- consistent camera angle and lighting
- consistent canvas dimensions
- optimized WebP versions
- subtle GSAP idle movement, floating, tilt, blink simulation, and hover parallax
- preserve accessibility with DOM buttons and labels

This is acceptable even though the characters are not yet true real-time 3D.

### Future 3D upgrade

Document a future asset pipeline:
- one rigged chibi astronaut GLB base
- shared skeleton and animation clips
- material, hairstyle, helmet, accessory, and body-scale variants
- SkeletonUtils.clone or equivalent
- optimized textures and shared geometries

Do not attempt to procedurally model ten production-quality characters from primitive Three.js geometry.

Reject the current symmetrical grid and center dividing line.

Build an asymmetrical orbital fleet formation with staggered depth.

Hierarchy:

Leadership:
- Khải at the visual apex
- Lan to Khải’s right, 8–12% lower vertically and approximately 92–94% of Khải’s visual scale

Sector prominence:
1. TH
2. VN
3. SG
4. MY
5. PH
6. ID

Since Thành handles both PH and ID, represent him as one dual-sector guardian with two orbit markers.

Composition:

- Khải occupies the primary command platform.
- Lan occupies a secondary leadership platform beside and slightly below Khải.
- Ice and Kla form the largest TH orbital cluster.
- Huệ and Hùng form the second-largest VN orbital cluster.
- Nguyên represents SG on the next orbit.
- Ngân represents MY on the following orbit.
- Thành appears at the middle of Nguyen & Ngân with PH and ID twin-sector nodes.

- straight four-person rows
- a rigid organization chart

- identical character scales

Use:

- varied depth and scale
- soft orbital arcs
- venture-specific atmospheric glow
- subtle constellation connections
- overlapping layers without covering faces or labels
